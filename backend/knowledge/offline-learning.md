# Offline-First Learning Resources

An offline-first learning environment makes approved educational materials available over a local network even when external internet connectivity is unavailable.

A school pilot can begin with openly licensed or institutionally approved resources, searchable documents and teacher-curated collections. Each resource should retain its title, source, licence and date of review.

AI-generated explanations, when introduced later, should cite the materials used and clearly communicate uncertainty. Teachers remain responsible for learning decisions, and learners should not be required to provide personal information to search public learning resources.
