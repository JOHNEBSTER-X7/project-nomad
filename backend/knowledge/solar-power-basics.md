# Solar Power for Community Technology Hubs

A solar-powered technology hub should be designed from a measured energy budget rather than a guessed panel size.

The assessment should record the power draw of the server, network equipment, displays and other loads; expected daily operating hours; local solar conditions; battery autonomy requirements; inverter losses; ventilation; and safe equipment installation.

The design should include appropriate electrical protection, qualified installation, battery safety procedures and a maintenance plan. Actual system sizing requires a site survey and qualified engineering review.
