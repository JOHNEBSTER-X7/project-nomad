# Community-Owned Digital Infrastructure

Community-owned digital infrastructure gives local institutions and residents meaningful influence over the systems they depend on. A sustainable deployment needs more than equipment: it needs local skills, clear responsibilities, repair options, transparent costs and a plan for replacement.

For a school or community technology hub, useful early questions include:

- Who is responsible for day-to-day operation?
- Who can administer accounts and approve content?
- What services must continue during an internet outage?
- How will the community report faults and request support?
- Where are backups stored, and who can restore them?
- How will equipment be maintained or replaced?

Local ownership should be defined with the people who use the system rather than assumed by the technology provider.
