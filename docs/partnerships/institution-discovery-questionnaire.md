# Institutional discovery questionnaire

Use in an introductory meeting with an authorised institutional representative. This is a discussion guide, not a request for personal or learner data. Record only organisation-level information and obtain permission before retaining notes.

## A. Institution and authority
1. What type of institution or educational community are you representing?
2. Are you authorised to discuss a possible exploratory assessment?
3. Who would need to approve further discussions, site access or any future test?
4. Which roles should be involved (leadership, ICT, teaching, safeguarding, privacy, facilities)?

## B. Current needs
5. Which digital learning or knowledge-access challenges are most important?
6. How often is internet access unavailable, slow or costly?
7. Which approved learning resources would be most useful offline?
8. Which languages, accessibility needs or assistive technologies should be considered?
9. What existing systems must not be disrupted?

## C. Infrastructure at a high level
10. Is there an existing computer room, server space or secure equipment location?
11. What broad categories of devices and connectivity are available?
12. Are power interruptions a material issue? If so, what is the typical pattern?
13. Is there an ICT or facilities team able to maintain local equipment?
14. Are there institutional restrictions on Wi-Fi, LAN equipment or external connectivity?

Do not request passwords, network diagrams containing sensitive details, IP address inventories or security configurations in this discovery conversation.

## D. Governance and safeguards
15. What privacy, child safeguarding, ICT security and procurement policies apply?
16. What approvals would be required before any learner-facing test?
17. What data must never be collected or processed?
18. What content approval and copyright checks are required?
19. What languages and channels should be used to communicate with parents/carers and the community, if relevant?

## E. Value and success
20. What would make a small, supervised offline-access demonstration useful?
21. Which outcomes matter most: access, reliability, relevance, educator workload, operating cost or local maintainability?
22. What would make the institution decide not to proceed?
23. What would a successful discovery phase need to deliver?

## F. Follow-up
24. Is the institution interested in receiving the technical concept and safety documentation?
25. Would it consider authorising a non-invasive site assessment at a later date?
26. Who is the appropriate point of contact for a formal written request?
