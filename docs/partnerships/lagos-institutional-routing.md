# Lagos State institutional routing — discovery phase

**Research checked:** 27 November 2026  
**Purpose:** identify official institutional entry points for an exploratory PROJECT NOMAD conversation. This is a routing note, not evidence of endorsement, partnership or pilot approval.

## Recommended sequence

### 1. Lagos State Universal Basic Education Board (LASUBEB) — first contact

Why first: LASUBEB's official contact page explicitly invites organisations to become partners. It is therefore the clearest initial route for a basic-education proposal and for asking how to obtain appropriate institutional guidance.

- Official contact page: https://lasubeb.lg.gov.ng/contact-us
- Email published by LASUBEB: `lagos_subeb@lagosstate.gov.ng`
- Telephone published by LASUBEB: `+234 815 086 5676`
- Address published by LASUBEB: 2 Ikorodu Road, Mende, Lagos State.
- Ask for: the appropriate partnerships/programmes contact and guidance on whether a non-invasive discovery discussion should be routed through a relevant Local Government Education Authority (LGEA), Education District or other unit.

Use the official website contact page or published official email. Do not rely on personal mobile numbers copied from third-party directories.

### 2. Lagos State Ministry of Education — parallel policy route

The Lagos State education portal publishes:
- Email: `mbse@lagosstate.gov.ng`
- Address: Block 5, Room 006, State Government Secretariat, Alausa.
- Telephone: `+234-1-4969061`
- Portal: https://lagosstate.gov.ng/education

Ask which directorate or desk should review an exploratory proposal concerning offline-first digital learning infrastructure and whether LASUBEB should lead the basic-education pathway.

### 4. Local Government Education Authorities (LGEAs) — school-level routing

LASUBEB publishes an LGEA directory with local authority offices and education-secretary contact details:
https://lasubeb.lg.gov.ng/lasubeb-lg-education-authority

Use the directory to identify the correct LGEA only after selecting a locality and receiving guidance from LASUBEB. Confirm contact details directly on the official page before use. An LGEA contact is not a substitute for formal approval from the institution or competent authority.

## Initial geographic approach

Do not choose a host school based on online ratings or convenience alone. During the first conversation, ask LASUBEB to recommend a willing institution with:
- a clearly authorised headteacher/principal and institutional sponsor;
- a relevant offline-access or digital-resource need;
- an ICT/facilities contact able to support an assessment;
- safeguarding and privacy review capacity;
- a secure equipment location and a realistic maintenance plan;
- willingness to consult educators and community stakeholders.

The first phase should be an exploratory discussion only. No school should be described publicly as a prospective partner until it has consented to that description.

## What to send

Attach or link only:
- the public PROJECT NOMAD repository;
- `docs/partnerships/overview-and-invitation.md`;
- a one-page summary of current prototype capabilities and limitations;
- the V0.8 site-assessment and readiness-gate documents.

Do not attach learner data, sensitive network diagrams, credentials, detailed security configurations or unverified claims about capacity.

## Requested response from the authority

1. Is this proposal within the agency's remit?
2. Which department or official role should receive a formal concept note?
3. Should the project engage LASUBEB, the Ministry, an Education District or an LGEA first?
4. What written permissions and reviews are required before a non-invasive site assessment?
5. Is there an appropriate public institution that may voluntarily consider an exploratory discussion?
