# PROJECT NOMAD — institutional discovery and partnership brief

## Purpose

PROJECT NOMAD is an open-source initiative exploring locally operated, offline-first digital knowledge and optional AI services for educational and community settings. Its aim is to help participating institutions evaluate whether locally controlled digital infrastructure can improve access to approved learning resources where connectivity is unreliable or expensive.

The initial geographic focus is educational communities in Lagos State, Nigeria. No host institution, implementation partner, funder or pilot site is currently confirmed.

## What exists today

The public repository contains an early software prototype with:
- A local web portal and API.
- Offline lexical and optional semantic knowledge retrieval.
- An optional, locally hosted AI runtime.
- Operator status and access-control foundations.
- Controlled import of locally staged content bundles.
- Early benchmark utilities and site/pilot readiness documentation.

The software is experimental. It is not certified, production-hardened or approved for use with live learner data.

## What a discovery partner would contribute

A prospective institution may help establish whether a future limited test is useful and appropriate by:
- Explaining its connectivity, power, digital learning and maintenance context.
- Identifying an accountable institutional sponsor and ICT/safeguarding contacts.
- Advising on curriculum relevance, accessibility and community priorities.
- Reviewing proposed privacy, safeguarding and operational boundaries.
- Deciding whether to proceed to a formal site assessment.

Discovery does not require the institution to provide learner records, grant network access, purchase equipment or commit to a pilot.

## Proposed discovery sequence

1. Introductory discussion with institutional leadership.
2. Non-sensitive needs and constraints discussion.
3. Review of the concept, prototype limitations and safeguarding boundaries.
4. If invited, written permission for a site assessment.
5. Joint decision on whether a lab demonstration or later limited test is appropriate.

Any site access, data processing, equipment installation or learner involvement requires separate written approval and applicable local reviews.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- No live learner data during initial discovery.
- No student data used for model training by default.
- No facial recognition, behavioural profiling or automated high-stakes learner decisions.
- Local AI remains disabled unless separately evaluated and explicitly approved.
- No external AI calls or content synchronisation without explicit institutional configuration and approval.
- The institution may decline or stop engagement without penalty.

## What we are asking

We are seeking an exploratory conversation with an educational institution or community organisation in Lagos State that is interested in offline-first digital learning infrastructure. The immediate request is feedback and, only if appropriate, permission to explore a formal site assessment—not a commitment to host a deployment.
