# Prospective host institution selection criteria

Use this framework to assess fit before proposing a pilot. It is not a ranking of named schools and should not be used to make assumptions about any institution.

| Criterion | Evidence to seek | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Institutional authority | Clear legal identity and authorised decision-maker | Ensures valid permission and accountability |
| Educational need | Documented offline-access or digital-resource challenge | Establishes a real use case |
| Safeguarding capacity | Named safeguarding lead and applicable policies | Protects children and participants |
| Privacy governance | Privacy/data protection contact and review process | Prevents inappropriate data collection |
| ICT support | Staff able to supervise and maintain a local system | Supports safe operation and recovery |
| Physical suitability | Secure, ventilated equipment location | Reduces theft, damage and environmental risks |
| Power context | Site-specific load and outage information | Enables responsible energy design |
| Community participation | Willingness to consult educators, learners and families appropriately | Supports trust, accessibility and relevance |
| Sustainability | Maintenance, ownership, training and lifecycle plan | Avoids abandoned equipment and recurring dependency |
| Evaluation readiness | Willingness to define measurable, non-invasive outcomes | Makes learning evidence-based |

## Exclusion or pause conditions

Pause engagement if:
- No authorised institutional sponsor is available.
- The institution expects immediate deployment without assessment.
- The proposed use requires collection of unnecessary learner data.
- Safeguarding, privacy or ICT risks cannot be reviewed.
- The site lacks safe power or equipment arrangements.
- There is no realistic local maintenance and incident-response capacity.
- The institution cannot freely decline or withdraw.

## Selection process

1. Conduct a non-sensitive discovery meeting.
2. Score each criterion as Unknown / Partial / Evidence available.
3. Identify critical blockers separately; do not average away a safety failure.
4. Obtain institutional permission before any site assessment.
5. Complete the V0.8 site assessment and readiness gates.
6. Recommend no-go, further discovery, lab demonstration or formal design review.

No institution should be named publicly as a partner without explicit written permission.
