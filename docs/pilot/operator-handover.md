# Operator training and handover outline

Before a supervised test, the host institution must identify at least two trained local operators or define an equivalent continuity arrangement.

## Training modules
1. System purpose, limitations and offline-first operating model.
2. Startup, shutdown, status checks and safe recovery.
3. Operator authentication, secret storage and credential rotation procedure.
4. Approved content intake, source allowlisting, review, import and index rebuild.
5. AI enable/disable controls, limitations and escalation of unsafe or unsupported answers.
6. Backup, restore, software updates and rollback.
7. Privacy, safeguarding, incident reporting and data-erasure procedures.
8. Physical equipment care, environmental checks and escalation to qualified electrical/network personnel.

## Competency evidence
- Operator can demonstrate normal startup and shutdown.
- Operator can identify an unhealthy API, AI runtime or knowledge index without exposing secrets.
- Operator can preview and reject an unapproved content bundle.
- Operator can follow the documented backup and restore exercise.
- Operator knows the stop conditions and incident contacts.
- Training completion is recorded by role and date; avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Handover pack
- Approved release identifier and checksums.
- Site-specific network and power diagrams held securely by the institution.
- Inventory and warranty records.
- Approved content source register.
- Backup schedule and restore test record.
- Incident and escalation contacts.
- Maintenance calendar, update/rollback plan and end-of-pilot data-erasure record.

This outline is not a substitute for qualified electrical, network, safeguarding or privacy training.
