# Initial controlled-pilot risk register

This is a starter register, not a completed site-specific risk assessment. Assign owners and review likelihood/impact with the host institution before any pilot.

| Risk | Potential impact | Initial control | Owner to assign | Go/no-go trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learner or staff data exposed | Privacy harm, loss of trust, regulatory consequences | Data minimisation, no learner accounts in discovery, access control, retention limits, incident plan | Institutional privacy lead | Any unresolved critical data-flow or access risk |
| Harmful or inaccurate AI response | Learner misinformation or harm | AI off by default, reviewed content, educator supervision, evaluated test set, clear limitations | Academic/safeguarding lead | Unsafe outputs not contained |
| Prompt injection in local documents | Model follows malicious content | Treat documents as untrusted, allowlisted sources, review content, test adversarial fixtures | Technical lead | Critical injection path remains |
| Unlicensed or unsuitable content | Rights infringement or inappropriate material | Provenance, licence and age-appropriateness review; withdrawal workflow | Content lead | Content ownership/permission unclear |
| Unauthorised network access | Exposure of services or institutional systems | Segmentation, firewall review, minimal ports, no discovery scanning, security tests | Host ICT lead | Network boundaries not approved |
| Power, battery or thermal incident | Injury, fire, equipment damage or outage | Qualified design/installation, protection, ventilation, monitoring and safe shutdown | Qualified installer / site facilities | Any unresolved electrical or fire-safety issue |
| Equipment theft or tampering | Service outage, data compromise | Secure enclosure, custody and physical access procedures | Site lead | No agreed custody owner |
| Service interruption | Learning disruption | Manual alternative, backup, recovery tests, clear support route | Operations lead | No tested recovery plan |
| Vendor/model licence or supply change | Loss of ability to use model/content | Licence review, pinned versions, alternatives and documented provenance | Technical lead | Rights or usage terms unclear |
| Community rejection or unequal access | Exclusion, mistrust or poor adoption | Co-design, accessible consultation, voluntary use, complaints route | Community liaison | Meaningful consultation absent |
| Scope expansion before evidence | Uncontrolled risk and cost | Stage gates, change control, written approvals | Project sponsor | Any expansion without re-approval |
