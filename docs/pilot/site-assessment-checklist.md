# V0.8 controlled pilot — site assessment checklist

Use this checklist during discovery with a prospective host institution. Completing it does not constitute approval to deploy. Record evidence, responsible owners and unresolved actions; do not collect learner names or unnecessary personal information.

## 1. Host institution and authority
- [ ] Institution identity and legal operating authority independently verified.
- [ ] Named institutional sponsor and accountable site lead appointed.
- [ ] Written permission to conduct a non-production site assessment obtained.
- [ ] Local education, child-protection, privacy and ICT requirements identified with qualified local advisers.
- [ ] Roles, incident contacts, escalation route and exit authority documented.

## 2. Community participation
- [ ] Teachers, learners (age-appropriate), parents/carers and community representatives consulted through institution-approved processes.
- [ ] Participation is voluntary and does not affect access to education or services.
- [ ] Accessible explanation of what the system does, what it does not do, and how to raise concerns prepared.
- [ ] Feedback, complaints and withdrawal process documented.
- [ ] Local language, accessibility and disability needs assessed.

## 3. Site and physical environment
- [ ] Secure, ventilated equipment location identified.
- [ ] Environmental conditions, dust, heat, humidity and physical access assessed.
- [ ] Equipment custody, keys, physical security and maintenance responsibilities agreed.
- [ ] Fire safety, electrical safety, earthing, surge/lightning protection and safe cable routes reviewed by qualified personnel.
- [ ] No installation work begins before written approval and qualified review.

## 4. Connectivity and network
- [ ] Existing LAN/Wi-Fi topology documented with institution authorisation.
- [ ] Coverage survey completed in permitted areas.
- [ ] Network segmentation and isolation requirements agreed with the institution's ICT lead.
- [ ] Guest, staff and learner access boundaries designed and reviewed.
- [ ] No scanning, interception or device inventory collection without explicit written authorisation.
- [ ] Offline behaviour and recovery from loss of backhaul tested in a lab before site use.

## 5. Power and resilience
- [ ] Representative load profile measured using suitable metering.
- [ ] Solar resource and shading assessed for the actual site and season.
- [ ] Battery chemistry, permitted depth of discharge, autonomy and replacement plan documented.
- [ ] Qualified local installer signs off system design, protection and installation.
- [ ] Safe shutdown, restart and outage procedures tested.
- [ ] No battery or solar procurement based solely on the repository calculator.

## 6. Content and AI
- [ ] Content owner and source provenance recorded for every approved content collection.
- [ ] Copyright, licence, age appropriateness, accuracy and local curriculum fit reviewed.
- [ ] Content moderation and update/withdrawal process established.
- [ ] Local AI disabled until a documented model evaluation and safeguarding review approve its use.
- [ ] AI output limitations, source citations and human oversight explained to educators.
- [ ] No automated high-stakes learner assessment, discipline, ranking or placement.

## 7. Privacy and security
- [ ] Data protection impact assessment or equivalent review completed where required.
- [ ] Data minimisation and retention schedule approved.
- [ ] No student data used for model training by default.
- [ ] No external AI or content sync without explicit institutional approval and documented configuration.
- [ ] Operator credentials, access control, backup, patching and incident response reviewed.
- [ ] Security testing completed by competent reviewers before any real learner use.

## 8. Readiness decision
A pilot may proceed only after every mandatory gate in `docs/pilot/readiness-gates.md` is evidenced and signed by the institution and project leads. Open safety, privacy, security or electrical issues are blockers—not waivers.

Assessment outcome: **Not assessed / In progress / Not approved / Approved for a limited test**.
