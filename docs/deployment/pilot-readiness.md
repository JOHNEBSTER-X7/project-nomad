# Lagos Pilot — Readiness Checklist

The Lagos State school pilot is proposed. No school, community partner, deployment date or site permission is currently confirmed.

## Site and stakeholders

- [ ] Identify candidate school(s) and authorised leadership.
- [ ] Consult teachers, learners in age-appropriate ways, parents/guardians and community representatives.
- [ ] Document needs, constraints, languages and accessibility requirements.
- [ ] Confirm a site owner and named local technical operator.
- [ ] Obtain written site permission and relevant institutional approvals.

## Technical assessment

- [ ] Measure connectivity quality and outage patterns.
- [ ] Assess safe equipment location, ventilation, physical security and environmental conditions.
- [ ] Measure power availability and estimate energy demand.
- [ ] Confirm local repair/replacement options and supply chain.
- [ ] Benchmark selected hardware against the agreed workload.
- [ ] Test offline operation, restart, backup and recovery.

## Privacy, safety and governance

- [ ] Map all data flows and data categories.
- [ ] Minimise personal data collection.
- [ ] Complete appropriate privacy and child-safeguarding assessments.
- [ ] Define access roles and account lifecycle.
- [ ] Establish retention, deletion, incident response and complaint procedures.
- [ ] Review approved content sources and model limitations.
- [ ] Ensure adult oversight and prohibit high-stakes automated decisions about learners.

## Pilot approval gate

Do not install or process real learner data until the school has approved the plan, required notices and consent arrangements are in place, risks have been reviewed, and a maintenance and incident-response owner has been named.
